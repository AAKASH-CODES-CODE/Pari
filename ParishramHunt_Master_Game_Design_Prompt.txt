2. CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP & MATCH PHASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A match consists of 3 distinct, synchronized phases:

A. HIDE PHASE (Duration: 60 Seconds)
   - Hunters are spawned at the Main Campus Gate on the Ground Floor and BLINDED / FROZEN.
     A dark frosted overlay ("HUNTERS BLINDED: HIDE PHASE IN PROGRESS") prevents viewing.
   - Props spawn inside the campus corridors and have 60 seconds to:
     * Explore classrooms and corridors.
     * Aim their crosshair at any object and tap "MORPH" to instantly disguise as that prop.
     * Find a stealthy spot (e.g., blend in as a chair beside other chairs, or a bottle on a desk).
     * Tap "LOCK" to freeze prop orientation and freely look around 360 degrees.

B. HUNT PHASE (Duration: 300 Seconds / 5 Minutes)
   - Hunters are released with full speed and armed with the "Chalk Blaster".
   - Hunters search rooms, checking suspicious objects.
   - Shooting a STATIC environment prop (false alarm) triggers a "WRONG HIT PENALTY":
     * Hunter takes -5 HP damage ("Recoil / Chalk Burn").
     * Audio buzz plays, screen flashes orange, crosshair shakes.
     * Prevents hunters from blindly spraying every classroom item.
   - Shooting a REAL disguise Prop deals blaster damage and triggers a hit-marker chime:
     * Prop flashes white/red.
     * Prop loses HP (scaling by size: Bottle = 25 HP, Chair = 60 HP, Bench/Desk = 120 HP).
   - If a Prop's HP reaches 0:
     * Fatal Shot Kill-Cam: Slow-motion camera tracks the chalk bullet to the prop,
       bursting it into chalk particles and revealing the defeated student.
     * The prop player enters Spectator Mode to watch remaining teammates.

C. ROUND OVER & WIN CONDITIONS
   - PROPS WIN IF: At least one Prop survives when the 300s timer expires.
   - HUNTERS WIN IF: All hidden Props are tagged/eliminated before time runs out.
   - Results Screen: Displays Winner Banner ("PROPS WIN!" / "HUNTERS WIN!"), MVP tags,
     survival score, and buttons for "PLAY NEXT ROUND" or "MAIN MENU".

3. PROP & HUNTER SPECIAL ABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. PROP TEAM ABILITIES:
   1. Morph Disguise:
      - Center screen raycast (max 4.5m distance).
      - If ray hits any valid prop, HUD shows "[TAP MORPH] -> Transform into Plastic Stool".
      - Swaps player mesh and bounding collision cylinder to match target prop.
   2. Rotation Lock (Free-Look Mode):
      - Locks the 3D prop geometry in place on the floor.
      - Disconnects camera rotation so the player can look all around without wobbling the prop.
   3. Whistle / Taunt (Cooldown: 20 Seconds):
      - Emits a loud audio whistle chirp from the prop's world position.
      - Awards +50 Survival Points.
      - Creates a subtle sound ripple, giving Hunters a direction clue.
   4. Decoy Clone (Cooldown: 30 Seconds):
      - Spawns an identical stationary clone prop at player's location.
      - Allows the real player to sneak away while Hunter inspects the decoy.
   5. Stun Flash Wave (Cooldown: 40 Seconds):
      - Emits an expanding chalk wave (radius 4.0m).
      - Any Hunter caught inside is stunned (blinded white screen and slowed to 25% speed for 3s).

B. HUNTER TEAM ABILITIES:
   1. Chalk Blaster:
      - 30-round magazine, semi-auto or 3-round burst.
      - Raycast trajectory with glowing white chalk particle trail.
   2. Radar Scanner (Cooldown: 35 Seconds):
      - Scans a 12m sphere and beeps with increasing frequency based on proximity to nearest prop.
   3. Sprint Boost:
      - Increases movement speed by 40% for 4 seconds (stamina bar based).

4. MOBILE TOUCH CONTROLS ARCHITECTURE (DUAL-ZONE INPUT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On touchscreens, touch conflicts between looking and moving cause major frustration.
Implement strict dual-zone partition:

A. LEFT ZONE (Screen X: 0.0 to 0.42):
   - Virtual Thumbstick (Dynamic / Floating or Fixed).
   - OnPointerDown inside left zone anchors the thumbstick base.
   - OnDrag calculates normalized vector (dir = delta / radius, clamped to 1.0).
   - Deadzone: 0.12f. Outer edge: 0.85f triggers Sprint.
   - Drives character translation in local camera forward/right directions.

B. RIGHT ZONE (Screen X: 0.42 to 1.0):
   - Touch Look Pad (Full right screen).
   - Captures drag deltas (deltaX, deltaY) per pointer ID.
   - Rotation:
     * Yaw (Horizontal): Rotates character or camera rig around global Y-axis.
     * Pitch (Vertical): Clamps camera elevation between -65° (looking down) and +75° (looking up).
   - Sensitivity: User-adjustable multiplier (default 1.0x).
   - Important: Action buttons (Fire, Jump, Morph, Whistle) sitting on the right MUST consume
     their individual taps without cancelling active camera drags from other fingers.

5. OFFLINE AI BOTS SYSTEM (ZERO INTERNET REQUIRED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For single-player mode, backfill matches with intelligent AI Bots (4 to 16 players total):

A. BOT HUNTERS:
   - State Machine: PATROL -> INVESTIGATE_WHISTLE -> SUSPECT_PROP -> SHOOT -> PURSUE.
   - Use NavMesh or Waypoint graph across Ground and 1st Floor.
   - When a Prop whistles within 20m, Bot sets destination to audio origin.
   - Suspicion Logic: Bot stops in a room, visually evaluates nearby props:
     * If prop is in an unnatural spot (e.g. stool in middle of doorway), suspicion rises.
     * After 1.5s delay, Bot fires 1 test shot.
     * If hit confirm, Bot continues firing until prop is eliminated.
     * If wrong hit (-5 HP), Bot learns it's static and resumes patrol.

B. BOT PROPS:
   - State Machine: SEEK_HIDING_SPOT -> MORPH -> SETTLE_AND_LOCK -> OCCASIONAL_WHISTLE.
   - Parses "map_props_layout.json" to identify plausible cluster zones:
     * Chairs near study desks, bottles on cafeteria tables, cycles in parking.
   - Every 30-45 seconds, rolls random chance to Whistle for bonus points.
   - If shot but alive, unlocks and sprints to another room to morph again!

Choose one of the following recommended architectures in Android Studio:

OPTION C: Native Android C++ / NDK with OpenGL ES 3.0 / Vulkan
- Libraries: tinygltf or assimp for .glb loading, glm for math.
- Benefits: Maximum performance, zero runtime overhead.

- Embed "preview/index.html" inside a full-screen hardware-accelerated Android WebView.

7. PROCEDURAL AUDIO SYNTHESIS (ZERO EXTERNAL SOUND ASSETS NEEDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Audio is generated programmatically using Android `AudioTrack` PCM streaming (16-bit 44.1kHz):

- Chalk Blaster Shot:
  * High-frequency pulse (980 Hz) decaying rapidly over 0.08s with white noise burst.
- Hit Marker Chime:
  * Pure sine wave double chime: 1320 Hz for 0.05s followed by 1760 Hz for 0.08s.
- Wrong Hit Penalty Buzz:
  * Square wave buzz at 110 Hz with low-pass filter over 0.22s.
- Prop Whistle Chirp:
  * Ascending sine glide from 1200 Hz to 2400 Hz over 0.35s with gentle vibrato.
- Countdown Tick:
  * Click impulse (2200 Hz, 0.015s) with stereo pan.
